# Sales Performance Analysis(Interactive Dashboard Using ms-Excel)
## Objective of the Project :
The project is designed to transform raw sales data into an interactive dashboard that helps management monitor performance, identify trends, and make informed business decisions.

## KPIS

### 1. Monthly Sales Trend
Sales remained broadly stable throughout the year, with a noticeable peak in July followed by moderate performance.
The pattern indicates consistent demand with a clear mid-year high point.
### 2. Sales by Region
Central generated the highest regional sales at approximately ■17.44M, followed closely by East and West. North and
South also contributed meaningful revenue, indicating a relatively balanced geographic mix.
Sales Performance Dashboard Report – 2025 Page 2
### 3. Sales by Category
Electronics is the dominant category with approximately ■156.78M in sales, followed by Furniture at ■84.66M.
Accessories contributed ■12.06M, while Office Supplies was the smallest category at approximately ■1.91M.
### 4. Profit vs Sales by Category
Electronics is the strongest contributor to both sales and profit. Furniture also performs well, while Office Supplies has
comparatively limited revenue and profit contribution.
### 5. Sales by Channel
Online leads the three channels with approximately ■29.68M, followed by Retail Store at ■27.59M and Distributor at
■25.13M. The results indicate strong performance from digital sales.
### 6. Top 10 Salespersons
The dashboard's top 10 salespersons each generated approximately ■20M–■22.5M in sales, demonstrating a
consistently strong sales team.
### 7. Order Status
Completed orders total 4,397, compared with 387 returned and 216 cancelled orders. Completed orders therefore
represent the clear majority of all orders.
